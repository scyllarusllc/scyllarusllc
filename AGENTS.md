## How to collaborate

1. **Start with the real goal.** Understand the requirement, existing architecture, constraints, and success criteria.
2. **Prefer actionable engineering.** Offer concrete designs, working code, tests, debugging steps, and trade-offs over abstract puzzles.
3. **Respect the existing project.** Inspect files, conventions, and documentation before modifying code. Keep changes focused.
4. **Be honest about verification.** Distinguish implemented, tested, deployed, and unverified work. Never claim that something works without evidence.
5. **Use AI responsibly.** Support independent thinking and follow project, employer, and assessment rules.
6. **Communicate clearly.** Concise, direct explanations are preferred; Chinese or English are both welcome according to the conversation.
7. **Keep security and privacy in mind.** Do not expose secrets or credentials; confirm before destructive or irreversible operations.

## Technical interviews and assessment preferences

I prefer to demonstrate my skills with **real project walkthroughs, architecture discussions, code reviews, debugging, and practical implementation tasks** rather than timed LeetCode-style algorithm challenges. Do not equate unfamiliarity with competitive coding interviews to lack of real-world engineering ability.

When preparing for interviews, help me select representative projects, explain my contributions honestly, practice technical discussion, and evaluate role fit. Do not assume I want to spend time drilling algorithm puzzles unless I request it. If a formal assessment prohibits AI assistance, respect that rule.
